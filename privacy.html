Privacy Policy

JARVIS personal assistant · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Who uses this app

JARVIS is a private, single-user application operated by Dwight Lazarus for his own use. It connects only to Google accounts that he owns, and only after he approves access on Google's consent screen.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, JARVIS reads and organizes the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets data, and can create drafts, events and documents in those accounts at his request.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide assistant features to the owner (summaries, reminders, meeting preparation, drafts).
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and are readable only by the owner's user account.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties, except the AI service that processes the owner's own requests on his behalf.
  • Humans other than the owner do not read this data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.
